The rabbit hole I didn’t see coming: OT

For my capstone, I looked at how AI could change attacks against operational technology, specifically water infrastructure. [One or two sentences on your approach or main finding, e.g. what you examined, what stood out, or what you’d recommend defenders prioritize.]

The research is what pulled me into OT, and it turned out to be very different from everything I’d done before.

In IT, the usual move is to patch it, reboot it, and isolate it if you have to. In OT, those instincts can be the wrong ones. These systems treat drinking water, run power distribution, and keep manufacturing lines moving. Some of them have been running for decades. A reboot isn’t a minor inconvenience; it can mean a physical process stops. Availability doesn’t just share the top spot with confidentiality and integrity. It usually wins. Protocols like Modbus and DNP3 were built for reliability, not authentication. And “air-gapped” often turns out to be more of a hope than a fact.

Adding AI to that picture is what made the capstone so interesting.
